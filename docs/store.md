# Eggruks Nat 20

Whenever anyone rolls a natural 20 with the official **Dice** extension, Eggruk the orc bursts onto everyone's screen with a choo choo, flying emojis and his seal of approval.

![Eggruks Nat 20](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aabotnes/eggruks-nat-20/main/docs/hero.jpg)

**Natural 20 celebration**

- Plays a sound and shows a pop-up for every player in the room when a d20 lands on 20
- Works with advantage and multi-dice rolls; hidden rolls are never revealed
- Each player can pick their own sound, image, captions and volume, or turn the pop-up off

**Roll history**

Click the d20 icon in the top left to see the last rolls from everyone at the table, written in dice notation (`3d6 (4, 2, 6) + 2`, `ADV 2d20 (14, 6) + 3`), with natural 20s and natural 1s highlighted. The history is saved in the room, and the GM can clear it.

**Requirements**

Enable the official **Dice** extension in the same room. Eggruks Nat 20 listens to its rolls.
